# SocialHub 🌐

**SocialHub** is a specialized personal productivity dashboard for Android designed for community managers, subreddit moderators, and digital leaders. It provides a professional mobile environment to draft, format, and organize official community announcements and educational posts with a level of precision that standard mobile apps lack.

---

## 🌟 The Goal
Standard mobile applications often fall short when it comes to high-quality content creation. **SocialHub** aims to bridge that gap by offering advanced text-formatting assistance and organizational tools, ensuring that community leaders can maintain high standards of communication directly from their mobile devices.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

*   **📅 Content Calendar:** Securely manage and visualize your managed platform's content schedule in one place.
*   **✍️ Advanced Markdown Editor:** A specialized editor tailored for long-form posts, ensuring perfect Markdown syntax for Reddit and professional layouts for Facebook.
*   **💾 Local & Cloud Drafts:** Save your progress locally on your device for offline work and sync it across your own devices via a secure private cloud.
*   **⏰ Posting Schedule Coordination:** Plan and coordinate your own posting times to keep your community active and informed without the stress of manual tracking.
*   **📝 Professional Formatting:** Improve post readability and structure to optimize content for your specific audience.

---

## 🛡️ Privacy & Compliance

**SocialHub** is built with a "Privacy First" and "Responsible Builder" philosophy:

1.  **Strictly Personal Use:** The application is designed for the account owner to manage their own specific subreddits and social media pages. It does not support multi-user management or third-party account handling.
2.  **Reddit API Compliance:** This app adheres to the Reddit Responsible Builder Policy, maintaining transparency and integrity within communities.
3.  **Secure Authentication:** We use official OAuth2 flows (Google, Facebook, Reddit) to ensure your credentials never touch our servers.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

*   **Language:** Kotlin
*   **UI Framework:** Jetpack Compose (Modern, reactive UI)
*   **Architecture:** Clean Architecture + MVVM (Model-View-ViewModel)
*   **Local Storage:** Room Database (Offline-first approach)
*   **Backend & Sync:** Firebase (Firestore, Auth, Cloud Storage)
*   **Networking:** Retrofit + OkHttp (REST API integration)
*   **DI:** Hilt / ViewModel Factory

---

## 📦 Installation (For Developers)

1. Clone the repository: `git clone https://github.com/Edmyjose/SocialHub.git`
2. Open the project in **Android Studio Quail** or newer.
3. Add your `google-services.json` to the `/app` directory.
4. Sync Gradle and Run.
